# MURL v0.8 held-out feedback-quality review

**Review date:** 2026-11-15  
**System:** Qwen3-4B v0.6 hierarchical multi-task  
**Scope:** all 47 archived held-out outputs; no regeneration or rescoring

## Rubric

The student-facing `grader_feedback` field was rated from 0 through 2 on four dimensions:

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mathematical correctness | Materially wrong diagnosis or correction | Partly correct but incomplete/ambiguous | Mathematically accurate |
| Specificity | Generic or unrelated | Names the relevant method or issue | Identifies the concrete error or valid feature |
| Actionability | Misleading or no useful next step | Direction is present but incomplete | Gives a usable correction or targeted affirmation |
| Tone | Inappropriate or punitive | Understandable but awkward | Concise, respectful, and student-appropriate |

The per-example ratings and notes are in [`v0.8-feedback-quality-rubric.csv`](v0.8-feedback-quality-rubric.csv). Ratings were performed as an AI-assisted closeout review against the frozen benchmark and reference solutions. They are not inter-rater-reliable human-subject scores and should not be described as a blinded human evaluation without independent human sign-off.

## Results

| Dimension | Score / maximum | Mean / 2 |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Mathematical correctness | 86 / 94 | 1.830 |
| Specificity | 73 / 94 | 1.553 |
| Actionability | 86 / 94 | 1.830 |
| Tone | 94 / 94 | 2.000 |
| **Total** | **339 / 376** | **7.213 / 8 per output** |

Twenty-five outputs scored 8/8, 18 scored 7/8, and four scored below 7. Thus 43/47 outputs met the review threshold of at least 7/8. The lower specificity score reflects concise affirmations on correct work; these often name the method but do not cite a numbered step because no correction is needed.

## Representative successes

- `int_trig_0095`: rejects the invalid power rule for `tan²x`, supplies `tan²x = sec²x - 1`, and gives the corrected antiderivative.
- `int_trigsub_0114`: reconstructs the trigonometric triangle and supplies the correct back-substitution.
- `int_pf_0141`: explains why long division must precede partial fractions.
- `int_def_0158`: identifies the transformed-bound error and gives the correct bound and result.
- `int_imp_0190`: explains that an infinite integrand does not alone imply divergence and states the finite value.

## Material feedback failures

| Example | Failure |
| --- | --- |
| `int_sub_0025` | Calls the work correct and misses the required back-substitution. |
| `int_sub_0029` | Rejects a valid alternative substitution and recommends replacing it. |
| `int_trig_0098` | Correctly notices the bad term but says the constant integrand should be dropped rather than integrated to `u`. |
| `int_def_0155` | Incorrectly requires transformed limits even though the student back-substitutes before evaluating. |

These four cases show why schema validity and label accuracy are not sufficient for deployment: fluent feedback can still contain a mathematically material error. Instructor review remains required.

## Interpretation boundary

This review is descriptive and post hoc. It did not alter labels, outputs, prompts, selection, or test metrics, and it does not authorize tuning on the held-out examples. A future study that improves feedback must use a new version and new untouched test set.
